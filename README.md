Assembly instructions:
1. put the axel inside the hole of the car for where the wheels are going to be placed (both axel/rods are the same dimensions)
2. Place the 2 big wheels in the back and cover it with the wheel cover
3. place the 2 small wheels in the front of the car and cover it with the wheel cover
4. glue the spoiler onto the the back of the car (make sure it's placed correctly)

Printing instructions:
1. Print with PETG
2. add supports
3. 10% infill
   
(I printed mine on Bambu labs A1 mini)

___

I made this mainly because I love cars and because I also wanted to explore making a sports car, I only made geometric type of cars till now and this projects helped me and I learned how to change that into nongeometric shapes through breaking down a cube like sketch.
